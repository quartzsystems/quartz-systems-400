                         Additional Message Information                         
                                                                                
 Message ID . . . . . . :   SQL6335       Severity . . . . . . . :   00         
 Message type . . . . . :   Information                                         
                                                                                
 Message . . . . :   STRSQL parameters were ignored.                            
 Cause . . . . . :   The requested session attributes have changed because a    
   saved interactive session has been resumed or an interactive session has     
   been recovered. When a saved or recovered interactive session is used, the   
   session attributes that were in effect for that session are used instead of  
   the attributes specified on the Start SQL (STRSQL) command. The date format  
   value has been changed from *YMD to *ISO.                                    
 Recovery  . . . :   If the session attributes from the saved or recovered      
   session are not desired, press F13 to change the session attributes.
