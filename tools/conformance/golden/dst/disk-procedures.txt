                     Disk Unit Problem Recovery Procedures                      
                                                                 System:  QS400 
                                                                                
 A mirrored pair keeps running on one half when the other is missing or         
 fails; the system shows A6001255 or A6001256 and the half is Suspended.        
                                                                                
 1. If the unit is back, resume mirrored protection (option 8). The             
    copy runs in the background and shows its percent here.                     
 2. If the unit is gone for good, attach a non-configured disk of at            
    least the same size and replace the configured unit (option 3).             
 3. An unprotected unit that is missing stops the IPL with B1001030;            
    attach it again and IPL.                                                    
 4. A unit of a device parity set that is missing or fails shows                
    DPY/Failed and the ASP stays usable. Replace it (option 3), or              
    attach it again and IPL; it is rebuilt from the rest of its set.            
                                                                                
 Every allocated page is checksummed. A page that fails on one half             
 is read from the other and rewritten; chkstg compares both halves.             
                                                                                
                                                                                
                                                                                
    Press Enter to continue.                                             Bottom 
 F3=Exit   F7=Previous page   F8=Next page   F12=Cancel
